🤝 A NOTE ON WORKLOAD AND BENEFIT OF THE DOUBT

Several audits this month were given the benefit of the doubt. Before flagging this as a gap, we want to acknowledge something important: the team has been managing the workload of seven people with only two people covering shifts on most days this month, and still came through with several strong, well handled resolutions under that pressure. That effort deserves genuine appreciation.

At the same time, the gaps we saw in follow up, tagging, and callback commitments are things our customers feel directly, through delayed updates, missed calls, and cases that stayed open longer than they should have. Going forward, every audit will be scored strictly against the defined parameters, and no further benefit of the doubt will be given.

We are confident that with the right staffing support in place, the team can meet this standard comfortably next month, and we will continue working on getting that support sorted. Thank you for holding things together through a genuinely difficult month.
